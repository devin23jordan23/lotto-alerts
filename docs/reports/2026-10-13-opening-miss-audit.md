## Cross-session check before changing confirmation

Saved first-bar confirmations between 9:35 and 10:00 were replayed against the next 30 minutes of sampled option bids across eight sessions (Oct 2–13). There were **62** evaluated rows with a valid quote path; **42** had no active alert within the following 2½ minutes. Only **7 of those 42** reached +40% before a −20% ask-to-bid dip; **37 of 42** had a −20% dip somewhere in the window. These are overlapping observations, not 42 independent trades.

For `OPENING_DRIVE` with score at least 84, **4 of 8** missed first-bar observations reached +40% before a −20% dip. The four successes include two META rows on the same day, plus AMD and AVGO. The losers include a SPY candidate scored **90.91** and a HOOD candidate scored **88.86**. Raising or lowering score alone would not separate them. A narrow rule combining strong stock pace, local volume and option acceleration would select the three winning names in this sample, but that cutoff is chosen after seeing the outcomes and spans only two dates. It is not yet evidence of a repeatable win-rate improvement.

## Changes and next measurement

SPCX is added to the default and required lotto universe, so a Railway universe override cannot silently remove it. The live alert gates, confirmation period, 0–1DTE requirement and score weights are unchanged. Nightly reports now label every qualified 9:35–10:00 first-bar idea with its 30-minute sampled bid peak, first +40% print, first −20% dip, and whether an active alert followed. This will let us compare the opening-drive exception against later sessions without using hindsight to change live signals. The next rule trial should target opening-drive continuation and bearish opening flips separately, and compare unique symbol/side episodes, 15- and 30-minute gains, and drawdown before first target.
