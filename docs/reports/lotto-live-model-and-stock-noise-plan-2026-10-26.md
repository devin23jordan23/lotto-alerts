## Why the stock WATCH stream can overfire

`stock_first.py` can send a stock-only card without choosing or grading an option contract. It looks back five minutes before 09:45 and fifteen after. Its `_new_leg()` allows another alert when the stock extends by 0.15 ATR and either the setup label changes or the last stock idea has ever experienced a 0.15% adverse move. The stored `min_stock_return` is the minimum **since that idea was created**, so once the small dip occurs, later 0.15-ATR extensions can repeatedly satisfy that side of the re-alert condition. A new price leg is not necessarily a fresh independent setup. No hard daily alert cap is desired; the correct unit for deduplication is a new accepted level/base after a resolved prior leg.

The Oct 19 full-engine replay of the candidate MOVER bypass found 12 of 14 priced MOVER ideas below +40% bid in 30 minutes; the exact per-chart audit is in `oct19-mover-failure-audit-2026-10-25.md`. Seven stock paths barely extended or reversed; five had meaningful stock follow-through but no eligible same-day contract reached +40% from that alert minute. The opening implementation falsely substitutes the open for a nonexistent 15-minute reference, and BASE_BREAK could accept a one-minute high/low as though a 15-minute base existed. The base-age defect is fixed on the unmerged branch and covered by tests. Its opening trigger still needs a validated replacement. These replay results are **not** today's live alert outcomes.
