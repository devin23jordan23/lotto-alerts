Repeated calls on the same ticker varied considerably; a fresh break or reversal mattered more than ticker interest alone. This is a descriptive pattern from one day, not a proven causal rule.

This is a retrospective setup, not a proven real-time alert or attainable return. The worker log contains no GOOGL potential idea; its exact candidate blocker is unavailable.

The unalerted 0/1-day chain check returned contracts for **GLD, GOOGL, SLV, SMH, XLE, and XLF**. MRNA, AFRM, UPST, AAOI, NBIS, and other large stock movers were in the universe, but the post-close Schwab chain query returned no October 5–6 contracts for them; their stock moves alone do not establish a 0/1-day lotto miss. XLE and SMH had interesting option trade-price paths, but sparse prints and missing historical bid/ask make them weaker evidence than GOOGL.

That later alert was a slow starter, while a fresh 13:24 alert on the same contract produced a better quick move. See [the TSLA case](../cases/2026-10-05-tsla-380c-opening-drive-curl.json).
